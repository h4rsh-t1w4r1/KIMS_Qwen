# Vertical playbooks

Starting points per business type. They shorten thinking, they are not templates: sample the palette and imagery from the
actual client, run the fingerprint check, and never give two competing clients in one locality the same direction. Every
compliance note is a flag to verify with the client, not legal advice (see `local-market-india.md`).

Format: mode, buyer, world and mood, hero, sections, CTA, trust, avoid, retention and SEO fit.

## Dentist or dental clinic (Mode 1, plus Mode 3 recall)
- **Buyer:** anxious, comparing two or three clinics on Maps, wants a named doctor and a fear-free first visit.
- **World and mood:** daylight, ceramic, quiet steel. Light mode. Reference ideas: a calm boutique-hotel reception, a daylit
  pharmacy. Tinted off-white canvas, deep ink or teal, one warm accent.
- **Hero:** real photo of the clinic or the doctor. Outcome headline about comfort and clarity, not smile perfection.
- **Sections:** doctor, services in patient language, what the first visit is like, reviews, FAQ (pain, cost, time), visit us.
- **CTA:** WhatsApp "Book a first visit", with call beside it.
- **Trust:** doctor's name, qualification, years, registration where required, real reviews.
- **Avoid:** instruments or open-mouth close-ups, before and after, "painless" or "permanent" guarantees, fear framing.
  Read `healthcare-vertical.md`.
- **Fit:** reminders and a six-monthly recall email (with consent). Pages for specific treatments, reviewed by the doctor.

## Clinic, physio, diagnostic centre (Mode 1)
Same family as above with `healthcare-vertical.md` as the governing file. Physio: calm movement and ease, no body before and
afters. Multi-specialty: findability and scale, a real building entrance, a department directory with clear next steps.

## Salon, spa, beauty (Mode 1)
- **Buyer:** wants to see real work and real prices, books by message.
- **World and mood:** the space's own materials (stone, linen, warm light). Light or softly toned, rarely dark.
- **Hero:** real photo of the space or a real result, with consent. Headline about how you leave feeling.
- **Sections:** service menu with prices, the team, a work gallery (real photos, consistent crop), reviews, booking, visit.
- **CTA:** WhatsApp booking with the service pre-selected.
- **Avoid:** stock models, guaranteed-result claims, filters that misrepresent results.
- **Fit:** reminders reduce no-shows. A rebooking nudge at the natural interval (opt-in).

## Classes, tuition, coaching (Mode 1, often Mode 3)
- **Buyer:** a parent or student judging seriousness and results.
- **World and mood:** study-room light, paper, chalk or whiteboard realism. Light mode, high legibility. Never dark.
- **Hero:** the teacher or the real classroom. Outcome headline in the learner's words.
- **Sections:** batches and timings (compact spacing), teacher credentials, method, honest results, trial class, fees, location.
- **CTA:** WhatsApp "Book a trial class".
- **Avoid:** unverifiable rank or selection claims. Results need the year, the source and the real numbers. Check current
  consumer-protection guidance for coaching advertisements.
- **Fit:** batch dashboard, fee reminders (transactional), parent updates. Data about minors needs parental consent and legal
  review (Mode 3 section 7).

## Perfume, attar, jewellery (Mode 2)
- **Buyer:** buying a feeling and an identity.
- **World and mood:** amber glass, mist, brass, a dim counter, silk. Dark is natural here. Keep the accent for CTAs only.
- **Hero:** Tier L, S or C. A single bottle on a stage with mist and slow light. Real product photography as the base.
- **Sections:** statement, notes as a pin sequence, scent or style finder (IKEA effect) ending in a WhatsApp enquiry,
  collection, visit the store.
- **CTA:** WhatsApp order, with store hours and map.
- **Avoid:** generated bottles carrying real brand marks, claims of authenticity guarantees you cannot back, fake scarcity.
- **Fit:** back-in-stock and new-arrival emails. Gift-season pages (Diwali, Eid, weddings) are real search demand.

## Real estate (Mode 2, plus Mode 3 lead nurture)
- **Buyer:** a high-stakes decision made with family, comparing builders and locations.
- **World and mood:** the project's real light and landscape. Often light and airy, premium dark only for a luxury tower.
- **Hero:** exterior approach (Tier L or S), a walkthrough chain only for flagships.
- **Sections:** the promise in one line, location advantage with real distances, unit types and plans, amenities, price
  transparency, construction progress, builder track record, site-visit booking.
- **CTA:** WhatsApp "Book a site visit" with a slot preselected.
- **Compliance flags:** show the RERA registration number where required and verify it with the client (MahaRERA in
  Maharashtra). Label generated renders "artist's impression" and match the approved plans. State area measures accurately.
- **Avoid:** generated amenities that are not in the plan, "ready possession" claims without proof.
- **Fit:** lead-nurture emails after enquiry (with consent), a brochure reciprocity artifact, pages per locality only if real.

## Mobile or electronics showroom (Mode 2)
- **Buyer:** compares price and spec, wants to know the offer is real today.
- **World and mood:** clean product light, the brand's own hardware design language, never imitating a brand's identity.
- **Hero:** the featured model on a stage (Tier L or S), colourways journey.
- **Sections:** featured model, spec beats (manufacturer-true), range gallery, EMI and exchange (true today), store.
- **CTA:** WhatsApp "Check today's price and stock".
- **Compliance flags:** authorised-dealer claims need authorisation. Respect brand trademark guidelines. EMI terms and prices
  must be current.
- **Fit:** price and stock live in an editable data file or on the retainer. Offer-alert emails with consent.

## Gym or studio (Mode 3)
- **Buyer:** wants to see the space, the coaches and a low-friction trial.
- **World and mood:** chalk, rubber, steel, one spotlight, or bright studio daylight for yoga or pilates. Dark is allowed
  for a hard-training gym. Choose from the actual space.
- **Hero:** real wide shot of the floor with a real member, outcome headline.
- **Sections:** programmes, coaches, timetable (compact), plans and honest prices, trial, reviews, visit.
- **CTA:** "Claim your free first week" or the owner's real offer.
- **Avoid:** transformation timelines you cannot support, shaming copy.
- **Fit:** full Mode 3: member dashboard, class booking, 24-hour reminders, 14-day re-engagement, training and nutrition pages.

## Bookstore (Mode 3, light)
- **Buyer:** browsers who value curation and community.
- **World and mood:** paper, spines, reading light, editorial type. Light, warm, never decorative.
- **Hero:** the real shop, the shelf, the owner's picks.
- **Sections:** featured shelf, curated lists, events, order by WhatsApp or a hosted shop, visit.
- **Avoid:** book covers without a right to show them: use publisher or distributor assets or your own photos.
- **Fit:** if the shop already runs on a hosted platform, use its accounts and email. Back-in-stock and new-arrival emails. Genre
  and school-booklist pages with real value.

## Therapist or counsellor (Mode 3, minimal)
- **Buyer:** vulnerable, needs calm, privacy and a gentle first step.
- **World and mood:** soft daylight, plants, quiet textures. Light, low contrast energy, high legibility.
- **Hero:** warm, honest, no stock smiles. A short statement of approach.
- **Sections:** who I work with, how sessions work, fees, practicalities, a short plain-language guide, contact.
- **CTA:** "Request a call" with one step and minimal data.
- **Avoid:** testimonials and outcome claims (professional ethics norms, verify with the practitioner), urgency, loss framing,
  free-text health fields. Include a visible line for people in crisis with the current national helpline, verified before
  publishing (India's Tele-MANAS is 14416).
- **Fit:** confirmation and reminder emails with no clinical content. No marketing re-engagement to former clients. Educational
  pages reviewed by the practitioner.

## Restaurant or café (Mode 1, or 2 for a launch)
- **Buyer:** deciding in a minute from a phone, near the place.
- **World and mood:** the kitchen's materials and light. Make the menu live text, not a PDF image.
- **Sections:** menu as text with prices, hours and map first, photos of real dishes, order links, reservations on WhatsApp.
- **Compliance flag:** show the FSSAI licence number where required (verify).
- **Fit:** pSEO for the neighbourhood is thin value unless the content is real. Focus on Google Business Profile and photos.
